# About Half Acre Club

> Fictional brand made for the Specs demo.

Half Acre Club sends one small growing project a month to people who don't have a garden. Most members rent. Most have a windowsill and not much else. Many have killed a plant before and quietly decided they're "not a plant person."

Each box is a single project, small enough to finish. A typical one: a compressed soil puck, a packet of seeds, and a project card with five short steps. The pea shoots box asks you to soak the puck, spread the soil in a container, scatter the seeds, water until the soil feels like a wrung-out sponge, and keep it somewhere bright. Sprouts show up in a few days. Around day ten, you cut a handful with kitchen scissors and eat them.

Members use whatever container they have. A reused takeout tub works fine, and the club would rather you used one than bought something.

The box ships the first week of each month to the contiguous US, costs $24 a month, and can be cancelled anytime. Dana Reyes started it in Philadelphia in early 2025, after filling her own apartment windowsill past the point of reason.

What the club is really selling is the first win: the moment someone who thought they couldn't grow anything is standing at the counter with scissors and a plate.
