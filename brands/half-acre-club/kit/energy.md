# Energy

> Fictional brand made for the Specs demo.

**Level: 3 of 5 (friendly, hands-on).** Scale: 1 is a gallery card, 3 is a friendly shop poster, 5 is a neon sign at 2am.

The reason: the audience is renters who think they kill plants. The brand's job is encouragement. Too quiet reads as a premium shop they can't afford; too loud reads as a deal site. A seed packet is the right volume: bright, clear, a little handmade.

## What a strong post looks like here

- **Paper you could pick up.** Cards, packets and prints stuck down with masking tape, at a slight tilt, with real edges. It should look like something on a kitchen counter, not a screen.
- **The headline cheers.** Big friendly serif (100–150px), broken over two or three lines, like a note on the fridge.
- **One handwritten moment.** A tomato-red note with a drawn circle or underline: the price, the day count, "Box 01".
- **The plant is the hero when there's a photo.** Crop to the green. A print with a cream border, taped, is the brand's way to hold a photo.
- **Texture is welcome but light.** A paper grain, a dotted tear line. No glow.

## Too far

- Neon, glow, heavy grain, or anything nightlife.
- More than one red thing, more than two tilted objects.
- Sale-site bursts and exclamation marks. Encourage; don't hype.
