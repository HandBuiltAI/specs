# Positioning core

> Half Acre Club is a fictional brand made for the Specs demo. Every fact below is invented and should be read as part of the fiction.

## What it is

A monthly subscription box. Each box holds **one small growing project** that fits on a windowsill, a shelf, or a fire escape: seeds, the growing medium, any small container it needs, and a one-page project card. Example: pea shoots in a shallow tray, ready to cut in about ten days.

One project per box, not a variety pack. The reason: the customer's problem is not a lack of seeds, it's never finishing. One project they can finish beats six they abandon.

## Who it's for

Renters in their 20s and 30s living in small city apartments. No yard, no balcony worth the name, maybe one dead basil plant behind them. They want to grow something but assume they can't, because of space, light, or a landlord. See `audience-renters` when it exists.

## The promise

**You can grow something real in the space you already have.** Small space is the starting condition, not an obstacle we apologize for.

## Why it's different

- **Small on purpose.** Projects are sized to a windowsill and a few weeks, so the first win comes fast.
- **Nothing to figure out.** Everything needed is in the box. The project card has no jargon; if a word like "germinate" appears, it's explained in the same sentence.
- **Renter-proof.** Nothing drills, mounts, or stains. Everything leaves with you when the lease ends.
