## Do / don't

| Do | Don't | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day six. Leaning toward the glass. | Look at these babies go!! 🌱🌱 | Specific beats excited. One emoji at most, and usually none. |
| Killed a basil plant? Same. Start here. | Even if you have a black thumb, you can do this! | Light acknowledgment; no labels, no apology. |
| Cut a handful. Put it on toast. | Elevate your meals with fresh, nutrient-dense greens. | Plain verbs and a real use; no health claims. |
| Fits on one sill. Nothing to drill. | Transform your space into an urban oasis. | Renter facts over fantasy. |
| You've got this. | We believe in you on your growing journey! | One quiet line of reassurance, at the end. |

## Captions

Lead with the concrete thing, give one useful detail, close with one calm line. Three to five short sentences.

> Pea shoots, day six. They lean toward the window because that's where the light is. Turn the tub a half turn each morning and they'll straighten up. Four more days.
