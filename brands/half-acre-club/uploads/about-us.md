# About Half Acre Club

Half Acre Club started with pea shoots in a takeout tub. We wrote the date on a piece of masking tape, put the tub in the kitchen window, and checked on it every time we went to make coffee. By the end of the week the shoots were leaning so far toward the glass that we turned the whole tub around. When there was enough to cut, we put them on scrambled eggs.

It was a little handful. We talked about those eggs for the rest of the morning.

Half Acre Club is a monthly box for growing one edible thing at a time. We make it for people who have always wanted to grow something but have about as much room as we did. Maybe you have a sunny sill over the sink. Maybe you have a back step that gets light in the afternoon. Maybe you’ve moved three times in five years and every time you think about buying a planter, you remember you’ll have to move the planter.

## What comes in the box

Each month we pick one project and send the seeds, the growing mix if the project calls for it, and a printed card you can keep next to whatever you’re growing. The card tells you how much room to clear, what kind of light to look for, and what to expect along the way. If a clean takeout tub will do, we’ll tell you what size to save.

Our first box is pea shoots. They taste a little like the inside of a fresh pea pod. You can cut a few for a sandwich, stir them into a bowl of noodles, or eat one over the sink while you decide what to make for dinner. That last one is how a surprising amount of our first batch went.

Some projects take longer or ask for a brighter spot. We tell you that up front so you can look around your place and decide whether the month’s box makes sense for you.

## What it looks like halfway through

A lot of growing advice skips straight from a packet of seeds to a beautiful bowl of something. We spend a fair bit of time on the days in between. Is the soil supposed to look like that? How much is “a little water”? What do you do when one side of the tub comes up and the other side seems to be taking the week off?

We take pictures as we go, usually on a phone while we’re standing in the kitchen. You’ll see the tape with the date on it and the patch where nothing came up. It helps to have a picture of an ordinary tub of pea shoots to look at when you’re standing over your own, wondering whether to water it again.

Sometimes a batch stalls. We try to give you one thing to check first, in words you can use right there at the window. And if you end up starting over, write a new date on the tape. We’ve done it plenty of times.

## Why we call it Half Acre

Half an acre sounded enormous to us. We had a strip of windowsill, most of which was already spoken for by the dish soap. The name stuck because it made us laugh, and because once we had something growing there, we started paying attention to that little strip of kitchen. We knew when the light reached it and when it moved on. We noticed which way the stems leaned before we’d even put the kettle on.

That’s still the part we like best. The box comes in the mail, you clear a little room, and for a while there’s something to look in on while the coffee brews. With any luck, some of it makes it to the eggs.
