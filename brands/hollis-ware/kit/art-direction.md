# Art direction

> Fictional brand made for the Specs demo.

## The camera

Shoot in the studio's north light, at table height, with the camera still. The reason: the pieces are about surface (speckle, bare rim, iron spots), and even, cool light shows surface honestly.

- **Light:** the north window only. No flash, no warm grade, no added glow.
- **Space:** leave a lot of empty wall or linen around the work. Crowding makes it look like a shop shelf.
- **Show the making:** hands trimming, clay shavings on the wheel, a tag tied on. That's the "handmade" message without saying the word.
- **Real use:** a cup on a table, plates stacked as they'd be in a cupboard. Never a styled breakfast.
- **Vary the set:** one wide (pieces by the window), one hands, one close detail (a tag, a rim).

## Photos and type together

Type sits on paper, beside or below the photo, never on it. Square photo edges, no rounded corners, no drop shadows. The one clay element (a rule, a number) sits near the type, not on the image.

## The starting shot list

| # | Shot | What has to be in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Plates by the window** | A stack of plates and two cups on linen, the north window at the edge, lots of wall. |
| 2 | **Trimming** | Hands trimming an upside-down cup on the wheel, clay shavings, apron behind. |
| 3 | **The tag** | One cup on linen with its kraft tag: "Batch 07 · 12/40 · $38". |

Reference photos for all three are in `uploads/`.

**Shot 1 is the test of the direction.** You should be able to tell the glaze, the scale and the light, and nothing in it should look arranged for a camera. If it could be a catalog page, it's the wrong photo.

## Never show

- Styled breakfasts, pastries, latte art, or flowers and succulents in the cups. June asked by name.
- Marble, plinths, or pieces on a shop shelf with price stickers.
- Warm, golden-hour grading or heavy filters.
- More than one clay-colored element.
