# About Hollis Ware

> Fictional brand made for the Specs demo.

Hollis Ware is a ceramics studio in Portland, Maine, run by one person, June Hollis. She works at one wheel in one room. A north-facing window there gives even light until about three in the afternoon, which is roughly when throwing stops and trimming starts.

The work is stoneware tableware: cups, plates and serving bowls, in a small family of quiet, speckled glazes. Everything is food safe and can go in the dishwasher and the microwave.

Work comes in batches. Batch 07 is forty pieces (fourteen cups, sixteen plates and ten bowls) in an oat speckle glaze, with heavier iron spots than the batch before. Each piece has a handwritten tag with its batch and its number, like "12/40". A batch goes online on a Sunday morning, about once a season. When it's sold, it's gone; nothing is restocked or remade.

A cup is $38, a plate $54, a serving bowl $140. On the first Saturday of every month, from 10 to 2, the studio is open: you can look at the shelves, watch June trim, and ask about the next batch.

None of it is meant to sit on a shelf. Use it, chip it eventually, and replace it from a later batch that looks a little different.
