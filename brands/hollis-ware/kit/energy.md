# Energy

> Fictional brand made for the Specs demo.

**Level: 1 of 5 (quiet).** Scale: 1 is a gallery card, 3 is a friendly shop poster, 5 is a neon sign at 2am.

The reason: Hollis Ware sells forty pieces at a time to people who already care about how things are made. Shouting would make it a shop. The empty space is the luxury.

## What a strong quiet post looks like

A quiet post can still be a strong idea. The boldness is in the decision, not the volume:

- **Radical space.** Two thirds of the canvas empty paper, the copy small and exact in one corner. The emptiness is the statement.
- **A crop that makes you lean in.** The photo cropped tight to one surface (the speckle, the bare clay rim, the tag) so it reads almost abstract.
- **One precise detail.** A single clay hairline, an index number, a tag line with middle dots. Placed exactly, and only one.
- **Scale contrast without loudness.** A large light serif headline against a tiny spaced tag line. Large means 110px in a light weight, not 300px in black.
- **Tone, not effects.** The photo can be softened, warmed slightly toward paper, or set as a calm duotone in paper and charcoal. Never glow, grain or texture.

## Too far

- Glow, rotation, stickers, paint strokes, bursts, anything that reads as a sale.
- Type filling the width. More than one clay element.
- Busy layouts. If you need a second device, the first one wasn't placed well enough.
