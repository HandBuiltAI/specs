# Layout system

> Fictional brand made for the Specs demo.

**The idea:** a post is the care card that ships with each piece: off-white paper, a lot of nothing, a few lines of type placed exactly. The empty space is the luxury; filling it would make it a shop.

## Grid and space

- Paper ground, 96px margin, an asymmetric grid: type hangs from one edge (usually the left) and sits low, in the bottom third. The top half is often empty, or holds the work.
- Leave at least a third of the canvas with nothing in it.
- Align everything to one or two edges. No centered stacks.

## Devices (one per post)

- **The hairline:** a single 2px clay rule, short (80–240px), above or beside the copy. It's the bare clay at the rim.
- **The tag line:** small spaced capitals or figures with middle dots, "Batch 07 · 12/40", in the hand or body font, in stone or clay.
- **The frame:** the photo held inside wide paper margins, like a print on a mat, never edge to edge on more than one side.
- **The index:** a small number in a corner (05, 07) like a page number.

## Type

- Headline in the heading serif, sentence case, 64–110px, light, never bold. Short lines.
- Body 28–34px in the body font, charcoal. Secondary lines in stone.

## Photos

- Framed with paper margins on at least three sides, or cropped tight to a single surface (speckle, rim, tag). Copy sits on paper beside or below, never on the photo.

## Never

- Rotation of any kind, rounded corners, shadows, or texture.
- Large type filling the width, or more than one clay element.
- Badges, bursts, or anything that reads as a sale.
