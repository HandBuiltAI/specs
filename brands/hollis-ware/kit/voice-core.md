# Voice core

> Fictional brand made for the Specs demo.

**The reference line:** "Batch 07. Forty pieces. Made slowly, sold once."

It reads like a tag. Every rule below explains why that works.

## Rules

1. **Write like the tag.** Number, material, price. "Batch 07 · 12/40 · $38" is the model. Facts are the warmth here, because they show someone counted.
2. **Short declaratives, full stops.** "Forty pieces." Calm, finished sentences. Never an exclamation mark: the brand's confidence is in not raising its voice.
3. **Materials over adjectives.** "Stoneware, oat speckle glaze, bare rim" beats "beautiful earthy tones". Name the thing; let the photo do the beauty.
4. **Plain about handmade.** Everything is handmade, so saying it is like a bakery saying it uses an oven. Show the process (trimming, the kiln, the tag) instead.
5. **Use, not display.** Mention the dishwasher. The pieces are for breakfast, not a shelf.
6. **Release facts, no urgency.** "Sunday, 9am Eastern. No restocks." is enough. Never "hurry" or "selling fast".
7. **Sentence case.** Tags are lowercase; everything else is sentence case. Never all caps.

## Long form

On the website and in longer captions the same voice allows a little scene: the north window, three o'clock, coffee served in a second. Still exact, still unhurried.

> "These are meant to be used, chipped eventually, and replaced from a later batch that looks a little different."

## Calibration

| Too far one way | On voice | Too far the other way |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Our handcrafted, one-of-a-kind artisan mugs are made with love and soul!! ✨" | "Batch 07. Forty pieces. Made slowly, sold once." | "SKU HW-07-C. Stoneware mug, 12 oz. In stock." |
| Gushing, "artisan", sparkle emoji | Exact and calm, a person counting | Catalog, no maker, no light |

## Never

- Exclamation marks.
- "Artisan", "handcrafted with love", "soul", "one of a kind", "curated", "elevated".
- Urgency: "hurry", "selling fast", "don't miss out", "limited time".
- Emoji.
