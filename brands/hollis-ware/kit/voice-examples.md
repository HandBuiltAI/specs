## Do / don't

| Do | Don't | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forty pieces. | A limited, exclusive collection! | Count, don't hype. |
| Dishwasher safe. | Delicate handcrafted treasure. | Use, not display. |
| Oat speckle, bare rim. | Gorgeous earthy vibes. | Materials over adjectives. |
| Online Sunday, 9am Eastern. | Hurry, these will sell out fast! | Release facts, no urgency. |
| Made by June, by the north window. | Made with love and soul. | Show the process; skip the feelings. |

## Captions

State the batch or the piece, give one material or use detail, close with the release fact or the tag. Two to four sentences.

> Batch 07 is forty pieces in oat speckle. The iron spots came out heavier this time; the rims are bare where the glaze broke. Online Sunday, 9am Eastern. No restocks.
