# About the studio

Hollis Ware is a small ceramics studio in Portland, Maine. Everything here is made by one person, June Hollis, at one wheel, in one room with a north-facing window that gives the same soft, even light from morning until about three in the afternoon, which is also roughly when June stops throwing and starts trimming.

The work is stoneware tableware: cups, plates and serving bowls, the things you reach for every day, glazed in a small family of quiet, speckled colors that come out of the kiln a little different every time. Every glaze is food safe, and every piece can go in the dishwasher, because a cup you're afraid to wash is a cup you don't use.

We make work in batches. Each batch is around thirty pieces, made over several weeks, fired together and released together, usually once a season. When a batch is gone, it's gone. We don't restock and we don't make the same batch twice, partly because the clay and the kiln wouldn't let us if we tried, and partly because we like it that way. Each piece carries a small handwritten tag with its batch number and where it falls in the batch.

Cups are $36. Plates are $54. Serving bowls are $140. Prices include the time it takes, which is most of it.

The studio is open to visitors on the first Saturday of every month from 10 to 2. You're welcome to come and look at what's on the shelves, watch June trim, and ask about the next batch. There is usually coffee, served in a second.

We're not interested in being precious about any of this. These are meant to be used, chipped eventually, and replaced from a later batch that looks a little different. That's the point of making them by hand.
