# Art direction

## The subject

Specs has no product photos. Its pictures are its screens, and the brand in them is someone else's. The reason: the whole idea is that Specs gets out of the way so a brand can be loud. A picture of Specs should prove that.

- **Show real output.** A screenshot of a post with "Why this works" pinned on it, the with / without toggle mid-flip, a kit folder open in a code editor. Crop tight, at 2× so type stays sharp.
- **Let the demo brands bring the color.** The pea green of Half Acre Club, the neon of Third Shift Slice, the clay of Hollis Ware. Specs' gray and white frame them.
- **Evidence in mono.** When a picture needs a label, set it like a source tag: `voice-core`, `from: about-us.pdf`.

## The three reference shots

1. **The start screen:** three demo cards, each in its own brand's colors, on the gray table. Three loud brands, one quiet room.
2. **A pinned post:** a Third Shift Slice post with numbered pins in the margin and the notes below, citing chunk IDs.
3. **With / without:** the same brief side by side; the kit version clearly better without a word of explanation.

## Never

- Stock photos of laptops, offices or hands on keyboards.
- Robots, brains, sparkles, glowing orbs or purple-to-blue gradients: the generic AI look the audience distrusts.
- Specs' own red used as decoration. If it's red, something needs a decision.
