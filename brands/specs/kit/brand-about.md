# About Specs

Specs reads the brand materials a small business already has, the logo, the website, an old flyer, phone photos, and writes down what makes the brand itself: who it's for, how it sounds, its colors and type, what it would never say. It writes that down as a brand kit, a folder of small files an AI can load one at a time, each with the upload it came from.

Then it uses the kit to make Instagram posts, and every post shows its reasoning.

## A session

1. **Start.** Pick a demo brand to see finished posts right away, or start with your own materials.
2. **Upload.** A logo, a website address, one PDF, up to five photos, and a note for anything else.
3. **The reveal.** The kit assembles on screen as it's read. Colors come from the photos, voice from the words, each item tagged with its source. Edit or delete anything.
4. **Fill the gaps.** Specs asks about what's missing or contradictory, one question at a time, using small purpose-built controls: pick a palette, place a tone on a dial, choose which line sounds like you.
5. **Make posts.** Pick what the post is for and fill a short brief. Each post comes with "Why this works", a with-kit / without-kit toggle and a PNG download. The kit downloads here too.

## Why it's built this way

Posts render from HTML templates filled with the kit's tokens, not from image generation, so fonts, colors and logos come out exact. The kit is plain text so it outlives the app.
