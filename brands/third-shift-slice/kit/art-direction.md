# Art direction

> Fictional brand made for the Specs demo.

## The camera

Shoot like a regular who just got their slice: phone in one hand, at the window or on the curb, at night. The reason: the people this is for only ever see the window after dark, so a daylight photo is a different place.

- **Light:** the pink neon outside and the fluorescent tube inside, both as they are. Green-white fluorescent is fine. No flash, no warm grade.
- **Keep the grain.** Night phone photos are noisy. That's honest; don't smooth it into a render.
- **Show the paper:** paper plates, napkin stacks, taped signs, the price board. They're the brand's materials.
- **Real people from behind or at an angle,** in scrubs or kitchen clothes. Never posed, never looking at the camera with a slice.
- **Vary the set:** one wide (the window), one close (the slice), one sign. In any three posts, don't use three close-ups of cheese.

## Photos and type together

Put headlines on the black, not over the photo's busy middle. If text must sit on a photo, use a paper label with marker lettering, like a sign taped to the glass. The pink is for one thing per post: the wordmark, a price stamp, or one underline.

## The starting shot list

| # | Shot | What has to be in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The window at night** | From the sidewalk: the pink SLICE neon, the painted banner and hours, the price board, one person in scrubs at the counter. |
| 2 | **The cheese pull** | A slice lifted off a paper plate under fluorescent light, napkins behind. |
| 3 | **The taped sign** | "OPEN TILL 3AM · WED–SUN" in marker on paper, taped inside the glass, the kitchen soft behind it. |

Reference photos for all three are in `uploads/`.

**Shot 1 is the test of the direction.** You should be able to tell the window is open, what a slice costs, and who it's for, without reading a caption. A photo that could be any pizza place in daylight is the wrong photo.

## Never show

- Bar scenes, drinks, or people visibly drunk. It's the end of a shift.
- Pizza styled on wooden boards, marble, or with basil leaves arranged on top.
- A dining room, tables, or delivery bags. None of them exist.
- Daylight shots of the window. It's closed in daylight.
