# About Third Shift Slice

> Fictional brand made for the Specs demo.

Third Shift Slice is a pizza window in Columbus, Ohio, that opens at 9pm and closes at 3am, Wednesday to Sunday. It exists for the people who finish work when everyone else is asleep: nurses in scrubs coming off a hospital shift, bartenders after last call, line cooks still in kitchen shoes.

There's no dining room. You walk up to a lit window under a pink neon SLICE sign, pick from three slices on a hand-painted board, and eat on the curb or on the walk home. Cheese is $4, pepperoni is $5, and the slice of the night is $6. It changes, and it's whatever Tavo felt like making, written on the board when he decides. Cash and card.

Tavo Ruiz runs it alone. He was a line cook for eleven years, so he knows what you want at 2am, and it isn't a salad. One person and one oven is also why there's no phone and no delivery.

Closing used to be 2am. Tavo kept seeing nurses walk past a dark window after their 2:30 shift change, so he pushed it to 3. The line painted on the wall, "Hot slice. 2:47am. Window's open.", came from one of those nurses texting a friend.

It isn't a bar thing. Half the people at the window haven't had a drink; they've had a twelve-hour shift.
