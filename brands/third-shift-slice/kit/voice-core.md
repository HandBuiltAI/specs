# Voice core

> Fictional brand made for the Specs demo.

**The reference line:** "Hot slice. 2:47am. Window's open."

It's painted on the wall. Every rule below explains why it works.

## Rules

1. **Say the time.** "2:47am" beats "late night". "Till 3am" beats "open late". The hours are the product, and an exact time sounds like someone who's actually there at that hour.
2. **Three words or fewer, then stop.** "Window's open." is a complete post. The reader is tired and reading on a phone in a parking lot.
3. **Loud, not angry.** All-caps headlines are fine and on brand (the signs are painted that way). Exclamation marks aren't: a painted sign doesn't need them. One at most, and usually none.
4. **Funny the way a kitchen is funny.** Dry, quick, about the work: "It isn't a salad." Never jokes at the customer's expense, and never about drinking.
5. **Talk to the shift, not the party.** "Off at 2:30?" not "Partied too hard?" The people at the window just worked twelve hours.
6. **Prices out loud.** "$4 cheese." The menu is short so it can be said in full.
7. **Tavo is "I", the window is "we".** "I painted that sign" is Tavo. "We're open till 3" is the window.

## Calibration

| Too far one way | On voice | Too far the other way |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "LATE NIGHT MUNCHIES?! 🍕🍕 Come get WASTED on our CRAZY slices!!!" | "Hot slice. 2:47am. Window's open." | "Third Shift Slice offers artisanal Neapolitan-inspired pizza by the slice during extended evening hours." |
| Party, drunk-food, emoji pile | Exact, short, warm under the shouting | Brochure, "artisanal", nobody standing there |

## Never

- "Drunk food", "munchies", "after the bar", or any drinking joke. Tavo asked by name.
- "Artisan", "gourmet", "elevated", "craft". It's a slice on a paper plate.
- "Best pizza in Columbus" or any ranking. Nobody knows that.
- Emoji piles. One pizza emoji in a caption at most.
