# Voice examples

> Fictional brand made for the Specs demo. Square brackets mark details that must come from the brief, never be invented.

## Lines from the brand's own materials

| Line | Source | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Sample lines by post intent

| Intent | Headline | Supporting line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Announcement | SLICE OF THE NIGHT: [flavor from the brief]. | $6 till it's gone. Window's open till 3. |
| Promo | OFF AT 2:30? WE'RE OPEN TILL 3. | Cheese $4. Pepperoni $5. Walk up. |
| Tip | Fold it. | One hand for the slice, one for your keys. |
| Event | [Event name]. [Date]. | Same window. [Detail from the brief]. |
| Testimonial | "[Regular's own words from the brief]" | [First name and job from the brief], [time from the brief]. |

## Do / don't

| Do | Don't | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Till 3am. | Open super late!! | The exact time is the promise; exclamation marks add nothing. |
| Off the floor at 2:30? | Rough night out? | It's a workday ending, not a party. |
| $4. Hot. Now. | Affordable artisanal slices. | Prices and temperature, not adjectives. |
| Tonight's slice: [from the brief]. | Our chef's elevated seasonal creation. | One cook, one board, plain words. |
| Window's open. | Come visit our location today! | The window is the brand; "location" is a chain word. |

## Captions

Lead with the time or the slice, give one plain detail, sign off with the hours. Two to four short sentences, all-caps allowed for one line.

> 2:47am and the cheese is still stretching. Pepperoni's $5, cheese is $4, the board says the rest. WINDOW'S OPEN TILL 3.
