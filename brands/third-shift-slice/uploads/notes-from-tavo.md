notes from tavo (typed up from a voice memo, sorry for the run-ons)

ok so the thing about the window is it's a window. no tables no chairs no delivery, you walk up, you get a slice, you go home or you eat it on the curb like everybody does. people ask can they call ahead and the answer is no, there's one of me and one oven and the phone would just ring.

who comes. honestly it's whoever's getting off work when everyone else is asleep. the nurses from the hospital up the street, their shift lets out around 2:30 which is the whole reason we pushed closing to 3 this spring, before that we closed at 2 and I'd see them walking past the dark window and I felt terrible about it. bartenders after close. line cooks, which, I was one for eleven years so I know exactly what you want at 2am and it's not a salad. a lot of scrubs. a lot of kitchen shoes. people who've been on their feet for ten hours.

the line on the wall "hot slice. 2:47am. window's open." came from a regular, a nurse, she texted her friend that exact thing one night and showed me and I painted it on the wall the next day. 2:47 isn't special it's just what time it was. I like that it's specific. we don't say "late night eats" or whatever, we say what time it is.

menu is short on purpose. cheese $4. pepperoni $5. slice of the night $6, that changes, I write it on the board when I decide, it's whatever I feel like making that night (last week was hot honey and soppressata, the week before was a white pie with too much garlic which people loved). cash and card both now. wednesday to sunday, 9pm to 3am. closed mon and tues because I have to sleep sometime.

what I don't want: please don't make us sound like drunk food. we're not a bar thing. half the people at the window haven't had a drink, they've had a twelve hour shift. also don't call it artisan or gourmet or "elevated", it's a slice of pizza on a paper plate. and don't say we're the best in columbus, I don't know that, nobody knows that.

what I do want: it should sound like the sign. loud, short, a little funny. like somebody yelling across the kitchen but nicely. "WINDOW'S OPEN." is basically the whole brand.

the pink neon SLICE sign was here when I got the space, I just fixed the transformer. the yellow is from the hand painted signs, I did them myself with a brush and a lot of tape. I want it to look like that, not like a chain. black wall, pink neon, yellow paint, paper signs taped up.

the old flyer from when we opened is still floating around, someone posted a photo of it last week. ignore the hours on it. and the prices. actually ignore most of it.
