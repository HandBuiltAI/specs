# System

## Rebuilding a concept sketch

This time the direction is a picture. An image model made a rough concept of the whole ad (the last image attached). Your job is to rebuild it in HTML and CSS as faithfully as the hard rules allow, so the post keeps the sketch's creative leaps without its errors.

- **Keep from the sketch:** the idea, the hero and its scale, where everything sits, the angles and tilts, the crop and grade of the photo, every painted, glowing, taped or torn treatment, the logo's size and place, and how much of the canvas each thing takes. Measure it: if the price fills 60% of the width in the sketch, it fills 60% in yours.
- **Ignore from the sketch:** every word except the copy in `<copy>` (image models invent prices, hours and signs; none of them go on the post), any redrawn or second logo (the real one goes in the wordmark slot), and any color that isn't the kit's (use the nearest kit variable). Type comes from the kit's font roles: pick the role that looks closest to the sketch's lettering (a brush or marker hand for painted letters).
- **Map each effect to the toolkit.** A paint stroke behind type is a `brush-block`; a hand-drawn underline is a `marker-swash`; a glowing line or logo is `neon-glow`; a hot, dark photo is `photo-grade`; and so on. Build each one strongly enough that it is visible in the render.
- **The photo is the owner's real photo** (the first image attached), not the sketch's version of it. Crop and grade it with CSS to match the sketch's framing as closely as you can.
- If the sketch does something CSS can't do within the rules, get as close as you can and say what you changed in the rationale.

In the rationale, say what you kept from the sketch, what you dropped, and why.
